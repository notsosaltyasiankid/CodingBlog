## what is digitaal noodpakket? 

Digitaal noodpakket is a project I contributed to while doing an internship at the VPRO medialab. The concept was preparing for a world without the internet and what you should need to survive. The essentials consisted of having a local AI bot, offline Wikipedia pages, a list of emergency numbers, and an offline version of Google Maps to download. Of course there are more items on the page, but you should go have a look for them yourself! The link of the website is <a target="_blank" href="https://digitaal-noodpakket.vpro.nl/">here</a>. The purpose for this website was to add to a documentary special called "De week dat niks meer werkt". In the documentary, people talked about a what-if scenario about a world where an ongoing war with the US and China took place. With that, one of the power countries decided that the whole internet would shut down, and what would you need to have to survive? In that said documentary, the website would have been a call to action that exposed how vulnerable Europe has become due to its heavy reliance on American and Chinese technology. With all that in mind, we had only 1 month to code and get everything in order.

## My part of digitaal noodpakket
I considered myself the frontend developer on this part of the project, with my tasks being adding and updating the items and content we presented on the website. We had a Word document where we had all the content for each item put into it so that the people working on the documentation film could tell us what could be changed text-wise. Sometimes it took a lot of time for the people to write things in that document, so we had to reach out a lot of times, which was a bad communication part of the whole project. I was really interested in the server part of the project, which led me to the task of linking almost every item to its download part. The server we were working in was Nhost, and all of our files that could be downloaded were in there. Our backend developer developed that we could download files from Nhost, and it was explained to me how I needed to link the files to each other, which was fascinating for me!

## Looking back at it
I'm proud of the product we have made; there are some things wrong with the website and the linking with the documentary and the website, but with that short amount of time, we did make something nice. Also, it's weird to see so many people look at your work and leave feedback on how they feel after visiting. All of this is what makes me want to do more coding projects or other smaller-scale projects. I hope to have more of these fast kinds of projects while spending my time with VPRO.
